**Core:** `inspect-robots view --video-url URL` makes each trial's "Run
video" stream a hosted http(s) MP4 instead of embedding one. `{log}`,
`{scene}` and `{trial}` placeholders give each log or trial its own URL (a
logs directory needs `{log}`). The page shows "Loading video…" until the
video plays and falls back to the frame flipbook if the URL fails to load.
Transcript rail seeking is unavailable for linked videos
([plan 0091](plans/0091-linked-run-video.md)).
