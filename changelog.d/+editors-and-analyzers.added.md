**Core:** record editors and analyzers, as in the paper. Raw logs and records are never rewritten after a run: an `Annotation` (judgement, note or grading error, with author, source, time and an optional `supersedes`) records each later judgement in an append-only `<log>.annotations.jsonl` beside the log, and `read_annotations` returns them after the judgements captured during the run, lifted from any log schema. `rescore` recomputes results from annotations through scorers that offer `score_annotations` (the `operator` scorer does), keeping other stored scores, so an unedited log re-scores to exactly its stored results. Analyzers are a new registry kind (`@analyzer`, entry-point group `inspect_robots.analyzers`) that receive read-only `AnnotatedLog` views and return an `Analysis`; `scores`, `terminations`, `digest` and `html` ship built in. New commands: `inspect-robots edit`, `score` and `analyze`. Logs record their epoch reducer in `EvalSpec.reducer`.
