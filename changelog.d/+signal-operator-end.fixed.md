**Rollout:** A supervisor can end an episode without waiting for the policy by sending `SIGURG` to `inspect-robots run` or `eval-set`. The signal interrupts a blocked policy call, discards any reply that arrives late, and ends the trial as an ordinary `operator_end` (scored, parked, logged), never as a cancellation. Motion already in flight finishes its step. `SIGURG` is ignored by default, so a misdirected End never terminates an evaluator unparked. Supervisors opt into two files through environment variables: `INSPECT_ROBOTS_END_SIGNAL_READY_FILE` receives the PID once the handler is installed (not for parallel `eval-set`, whose worker-thread trials ignore End), and `INSPECT_ROBOTS_END_SIGNAL_ACK_FILE` receives it once a main-thread trial has ended because of End, after which no further action can start. A refusal because another component owns `SIGURG` warns. A keypress End is still read between steps, so it first executes the reply the policy was waiting on.
