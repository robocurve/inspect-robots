**Agent plugin (0.30.0):** every observation that follows a move now narrates how much of the motion played and the readback's largest remaining offset from the target (or `Target reached.`), in every image mode rather than only behind a `take_pic`. Observations carrying an embodiment `motion_report` extra (a mapping whose `lines` are plain-language strings, such as the YAM plugin's per-arm IK diagnosis) have those lines rendered after the approver line, so the model is told why a move fell short.
